## 2. Reproducibility (details in `verification/README.md`)

* Every script was rerun from scratch with the fixed code into `verification/full_rerun_2026-11-18/` (about 2 h on 2 cores), except the long density-problem ensembles (`exp_dbto_ensemble.py`, `exp_dbto_part2.py`), the trust-constr multiscale runs (`exp_hbto_runs.py`, never run to completion by design) and the TensorFlow cross-check.
* Deterministic results reproduce to round-off: ground truth, surrogate truth (≤ 2.3e-14 against the document), all 60 newer networks (bit-identical), all 31 multiscale optimisation runs (identical).
* An independent finite-element re-implementation (written separately from the package) reproduces the start-design compliances, the authors' saved start-design sensitivities, and every true compliance of the authors' designs and of the optimisation runs.
* Not bit-reproducible across machines: round-off-sized numbers, the 1-D architecture study (training differs between Mac and Linux), the chaotic density-problem runs, and timings. Conclusions are unchanged.
* `results/` keeps the uploaded files, except: `surrogate_truth.json` (rerun with the fixed script; all earlier values unchanged to 7.6e-11), `patch_check.json`/`.npz` (the uploaded files held 2 of 19 runs from an interrupted run; replaced by the complete rerun, which matches the uploaded `patch_check_summary.json`), and the new `final_designs_rescored.json` and `published_long.json`. `dbto_ensemble.npz` (1 run) is also left over from an interrupted run; `make_figures.py` uses `dbto_ensemble_part1.npz`.

## 3. Final numbers

See `COMPARISON_WITH_PAPER.md` for the full comparison with the paper. In short:
* The paper's printed numbers (Table 1 errors, Figs. 8 and 11) come out of the authors' saved models and designs exactly; NNJ, CSM and AD agree to about 1e-15.
* The paper's CFD discrepancy is a float32 bug (error 1.65e-2 → 2.3e-10 in float64), and its multiscale compliances are 8.5–10.3 % too optimistic.
* The improved network gives designs with 3.9–4.6 % lower true compliance than the paper's, within 0.1 % of the reference optimum. Against the paper's network with the same optimizer the gain is 3.8 % at 3,000 iterations and about 2 % once that network has plateaued (≈ 80.5 from 10,000 to 30,000 iterations, never converging).
* Density problem: no measurable gain; repeated runs of one method land 1.5 % apart.
